## The one thing to get right before saying anything public

Donating your own money to them needs no permission -- just do it.

Publicly saying "a portion of what Same Heart raises goes to Covenant
House" is different: that's using their name to solicit funds, and their
own fundraising guidelines say anyone raising money in their name needs to
be identifiable, which in practice means getting their sign-off first.
https://covenanthousetoronto.ca/donor-information/fundraising-guidelines/

So: talk to them before the site implies any formal tie to them.
