# Same Heart — Card Art Checklist

Six tiers, grey to platinum. Each card below has a **name**, a **scene** (what's actually in the frame), and a **location/setting** to shoot or paint toward. Check boxes off as art gets made. Feel free to swap, rename, or cut — this is a running list, not a locked spec.

Running theme across all tiers: every card is a *moment*, not a logo. Same Heart's whole thing is connection — so every scene should have either two people, a person reaching toward someone, or a lone moment that's clearly *about to become* connection (a porch light on, a door cracked open, an empty second chair).

---

## GREY — Common ("Everyday arrivals")
Plain, warm, numerous — the cards almost everyone gets early. Low drama, high relatability. Think: the small act, not the big one.

- [ ] **Open Door** — a screen door propped open with a shoe, porch light on, dusk
- [ ] **First Coffee** — two mugs on a kitchen table, one still steaming, one chair empty (waiting, not abandoned)
- [ ] **Just Checking In** — a phone lock screen, one text bubble: "hey, you good?"
- [ ] **Still Here** — a chair pulled up next to a hospital bed / recovery room, hand resting on the rail
- [ ] **Porch Light** — a single bulb over a door, snow falling, Ontario winter
- [ ] **Morning Commute** — TTC subway platform, two strangers, one offering an earbud
- [ ] **Steady Hands** — close-up, one person tying another's shoelace or steadying a ladder
- [ ] **Quiet Company** — two people on a bench, not talking, just sitting
- [ ] **Shared Umbrella** — rain, one umbrella, two people under it, King St or Yonge St backdrop
- [ ] **The Long Shift** — a City of Toronto waste collection truck at dawn, driver nodding to a neighbour out walking a dog (a quiet nod to your own day job)

**Location ideas:** Toronto porches/triplexes, TTC platforms, Tim Hortons booth, snowy driveway, a City work yard at 5am.

---

## BLUE — Uncommon ("Water and reflection")
Calmer, more composed. Water and stillness as the visual thread — Ontario's lakes and shoreline.

- [ ] **Lake Ontario Dawn** — fog over the lake, one figure on the shore
- [ ] **Rainwindow** — condensation on glass, a handprint wiped clear, city lights blurred beyond
- [ ] **Canoe at First Light** — Algonquin-style lake, mist, one paddle resting across the hull
- [ ] **Niagara Mist** — the Falls from a distance, rainbow faint in the spray
- [ ] **Harbourfront Hush** — Toronto Islands ferry dock, empty bench, gulls
- [ ] **Tideline Talk** — two sets of footprints walking the same shoreline, side by side
- [ ] **The Ferry Home** — silhouette on the Island ferry, skyline behind
- [ ] **Still Water** — a frozen pond, a single skate mark cut across it

**Location ideas:** Toronto Islands, the Beaches boardwalk, Niagara, Algonquin Park, Scarborough Bluffs.

---

## PURPLE — Rare ("Dusk, and the four Paths")
Twilight tones. This is the tier where the four Paths (**Guardian / Seeker / Weaver / Flame**) each get a signature card — give each Path its own visual language so people recognize "their" card on sight.

- [ ] **Guardian's Watch** — someone standing at a doorway at night, arms crossed, calm — not blocking, just present
- [ ] **Seeker's Trailhead** — a forest path forking, one trail marked, one not, headlamp beam
- [ ] **Weaver's Loom** — hands working thread/rope/yarn into something, string literally becoming a shape (nod to "Heart Strings")
- [ ] **Flame's Ember** — a single match or campfire coal glowing in near-dark, held close
- [ ] **Dusk Over the 6ix** — Toronto skyline at blue hour, CN Tower lit, seen from a rooftop
- [ ] **Aurora Whisper** — faint northern lights over a Northern Ontario treeline (Muskoka/Temagami-style)
- [ ] **The Storm Breaking** — clouds splitting over Lake Ontario, one shaft of light through
- [ ] **Lantern Line** — a row of hand-carried lanterns at a night walk/vigil (Take Back Toronto-adjacent, without naming it directly)

**Location ideas:** A Toronto rooftop at dusk, a Northern Ontario treeline, a forest trailhead, a vigil/march at night.

---

## RED — Epic ("Breakthrough")
Intensity, courage, the moment after the hard part. Action and heat in the palette (not violence — *effort*).

- [ ] **Take Back the Night** — a crowd carrying lanterns/signs down a street at night, lit from below
- [ ] **Scar and Story** — a close-up of hands or forearms, visible scar, not hidden, not dramatized — just shown
- [ ] **Summit Push** — a lone hiker near a ridge top, wind-whipped, almost there
- [ ] **After the Storm** — storm clouds breaking apart over a city skyline, light breaking through
- [ ] **CN Tower Strike** — lightning over the CN Tower, long exposure
- [ ] **Rise Again** — someone getting up off the mat/ground, hand reaching down to help
- [ ] **Red Line** — a single red thread/string pulled taut across a dark frame (Heart Strings motif, escalated)
- [ ] **The Last Mile** — a runner alone on an empty road at the edge of a marathon, exhausted, still moving

**Location ideas:** Downtown Toronto street at night, a Niagara Escarpment ridge, CN Tower from across the harbour, a track/road at dawn.

---

## GOLD — Legendary (holographic foil finish)
The mythic tier before Platinum. Holographic treatment on all of these — cosmic/galaxy motifs mixed with real Canadian landmarks, since the site's own Galaxy page already uses a starfield theme.

- [ ] **Northern Lights Vow** — full aurora over the Canadian Shield, two silhouettes watching together
- [ ] **Golden Hour, Toronto Islands** — skyline lit gold from the island side, water like glass
- [ ] **Lighthouse Beam** — a Great Lakes lighthouse cutting fog, beam sweeping toward the viewer
- [ ] **Carved From the Same Wood** — a hand-carved wooden heart, grain visible, held in two palms (direct nod to your own woodworking — this one's personal)
- [ ] **Doorway to the Galaxy** — a literal starfield opening behind an ordinary doorway (ties straight into the site's Galaxy page)
- [ ] **Heart String Constellation** — a constellation drawn not from stars but from glowing thread, connecting point to point
- [ ] **The Hundred Hands** — a collage-style card, many hands from many people all reaching toward one centre point

**Treatment note:** foil/holo shimmer should read as *warmth catching light*, not sci-fi chrome — keep it earthy-gold, not silver.

---

## PLATINUM — "Unbreakable" (post–Level 100, real-world tie-in)
These unlock only past Level 100, and each one should carry an actual real-world hook — a code, a coordinate, a hidden object — so claiming the card is the start of something, not the end of it. This is also the tier that could crossover with IRL streaming: a platinum reveal live on camera, with the real-world treasure found in the same stream.

- [ ] **The Unbreakable Heart** — the flagship card: a cracked stone or ceramic heart, repaired with visible gold seams (kintsugi-style) — "broken and still whole"
- [ ] **The Carver's Mark** — ties to a real hand-carved wooden token you make and physically hide somewhere; the card carries a cryptic clue to its location
- [ ] **Coordinates to Nowhere** — the card literally prints a lat/long on its face; what's actually at those coordinates is the real-world payoff
- [ ] **The Hundredth Mile** — a milestone card awarded exactly at Level 100, numbered/serialized per recipient (card #1, #2, #3…)
- [ ] **Founder's Compass** — a limited, possibly hand-signed card, tied to you personally — the rarest "meet the founder" card
- [ ] **Take Back Toronto: Year One** — a commemorative card tied to a real event date, limited to people who were actually there or who checked in that night
- [ ] **The Same Blood** — two wooden hearts carved from the same single block of wood, split apart — the card only "completes" when paired with someone else's matching half

**Production note:** platinum cards are the ones worth treating as limited runs (numbered, dated, or event-gated) rather than always-available unlocks — that's what makes "unbreakable" mean something instead of just being a tier name.

---

## Cross-cutting ideas worth keeping in your back pocket
- A **seasonal variant** of a Grey or Blue card (same scene, winter vs. summer) as a cheap way to double the catalog without new concepts.
- A **Path-colour accent** on every card border (a thin guardian/seeker/weaver/flame-coloured line), so even Grey cards visually sort by Path once someone has one.
- Save your **wood-sculpture photography** specifically for Gold and Platinum — it's the most distinctive, hardest-to-fake visual asset you have, and it's literally yours.
